# Typhoon damage (not included)

The opt-in typhoon draw (`infinity_gem/stochastic.py`, `DisasterDraw.from_data()`; `scenarios/stochastic_outlook.py --disasters`; check SD1) is calibrated on a small table, `PAGASA_ARTC_typhoon_damage_events_and_seasons_2017-2023.csv`, compiled from PAGASA's Annual Reports on Philippine Tropical Cyclones (2017-2023; damage to agriculture and infrastructure as reported by NDRRMC and OCD). The reports say that no part of them may be reproduced without PAGASA's written permission, so the table is not in this repository and check SD1 reports NOT RUN without it.

To compile it, download the reports from PAGASA (https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/tamss/weather/tcsummary/) and save a CSV in this folder with `#` comment lines for the sources and the columns `record` (`event`, `season` or `frequency`), `season`, `name`, `total_php_bn`, `agriculture_php_bn`, `infrastructure_php_bn` and, for the `frequency` row, `count` and `seasons` (destructive cyclones, those whose names PAGASA decommissioned, and the seasons they cover), then `source` and `note`. `DisasterDraw.from_data()` reads those columns; `validation/stochastic_disasters/test_disaster_draw.py` recomputes the calibration from the file.
